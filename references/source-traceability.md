## Adapted decisions

- Treat 20-word and 25-word limits as review targets rather than hard limits.
- Prefer simple verb forms without prohibiting necessary perfect or progressive aspect.
- Preserve established software verbs and `-ing` technical nouns through the subject-field exception model.
- Extend fixed-text protection to all machine-significant identifiers and externally defined software terms.
- Treat canonical vocabulary defined by a governing source as binding in downstream artifacts.
- Preserve standard-defined normative keywords over ordinary STE substitutions.
- Replace the aerospace warning/caution taxonomy with the applicable project or domain taxonomy.
- Apply a light profile to selected technical conversations rather than controlled-document formality.
- Use minimum intervention for revisions and leave already-clear text unchanged.
- Apply the code-related prose profile to comment removal. Never remove an untargeted comment or polish narration into shorter narration.
